ESP32 Rover Wheel Control

2026

R.O.V.E.R. subsystem · Embedded prototype

Wrote the embedded drive controller that converts two independent RC receiver channels into proportional, bidirectional commands for the rover’s left and right motors.

  • Drives an XY-160D at 2 kHz with 8-bit PWM, constrained duty output, and a 60 µs neutral deadband to prevent motor creep.
  • Auto-calibrates both stick centers from 80 samples, rejects invalid pulses, and stops both motors after a 250 ms signal-loss failsafe.
